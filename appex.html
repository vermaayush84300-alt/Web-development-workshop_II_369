ACCIDENT PREVENTION

Multi-Layer Accident Prevention System

6 integrated AI systems working together to predict, warn, and prevent road accidents before they happen — reducing collision rates by up to 73%.

⚠️

Predictive Hazard Detection

Road surface sensors detect ice, oil, water, and debris. AI correlates patterns to flag danger zones before vehicles arrive, broadcasting V2X warnings to all nearby cars.

73% fewer accidents in deployed zones
🌡️

Black Ice Early Warning

Temperature sensors detect sub-zero surface conditions 40 minutes before ice forms. Road paint shifts colour to warn drivers. Dynamic speed limits auto-reduce via V2X.

40-min advance warning · /api/temperature
🚗

Tailgating & Speed AI

Pressure sensors calculate inter-vehicle spacing at 10,000 samples/sec. AI identifies dangerous following distances and broadcasts personalised V2X speed advisories to each vehicle.

Real-time V2X · <50ms alert latency
🧠

Fatigue Zone Mapping

Long straight sections with low accident history are analysed for driver fatigue risk. Smart paint changes texture and luminance to create subliminal alertness cues at night.

NeuralFatigue model · 88% detection rate
🦺

Pedestrian Safety Zones

Pressure + IR sensor fusion detects pedestrians at crossings. Road glows to warn drivers while V2X halts approaching autonomous vehicles and warns human-driven cars instantly.

Zero pedestrian fatalities in test zones
🌀

Pothole & Crack Alert

Graphene strain sensors detect micro-fractures forming over weeks. AI schedules maintenance 30 days before a dangerous pothole develops — preventing tyre failures and accidents.

30-day advance maintenance scheduling

ARCHITECTURE

Smart Paint Layer System

Hover each layer to reveal full technical specifications and API endpoints.

01 TOP LAYER

Protective Reactive Layer

Self-healing polymer with photoluminescent pigments. Thermochromic — changes colour on hazard or ice detection.

◈ Thickness: 0.8–1.2 mm
◈ Self-heal time: <4 hours
◈ UV luminescence: 850 nm
◈ Range: −40°C to +85°C
◈ Thermochromic shift: <2°C trigger
◈ API: /api/layer/01/status

02 SENSOR LAYER

Nano Sensor Network

2,400 quantum-dot MEMS sensors per m² measuring 47 parameters. Accident detection in <200ms.

◈ Density: 2,400/m²
◈ Sample rate: 10,000 Hz
◈ Power: 0.3µW per node
◈ Bus: 5G mesh / LoRa
◈ Accident trigger: <200ms
◈ API: /api/sensor/realtime

03 POWER LAYER

Conductive Graphene

Few-layer graphene as power bus, data backbone, and piezoelectric energy harvester for the full sensor array.

◈ Conductivity: 10⁸ S/m
◈ Yield: 3.2 kW/km/lane
◈ Tensile: 130 GPa
◈ EV wireless: 11 kW
◈ Emergency backup power
◈ API: /api/energy/harvest

04 BASE LAYER

Base Adhesive Layer

Nano-silica reinforced polymer with thermal regulation channels and chemical anchoring to substrate.

◈ Bond strength: 4.8 MPa
◈ Thermal channels: 40 mm
◈ Lifespan: 15+ years
◈ Cure time: 45 minutes
◈ Substrate: asphalt/concrete
◈ API: /api/layer/04/integrity
